Product Care Tips

Protect your gear for longer performance

Simple care habits help preserve fit, color, and comfort so your sportswear stays ready for every workout.

Wash cold

Use cold water to help preserve fabric stretch, color, and overall performance.

Turn inside out

Wash garments inside out to reduce friction and protect logos, prints, and surface texture.

Use mild detergent

Choose a gentle detergent and avoid fabric softeners so moisture control stays effective.

Air dry naturally

Let your gear dry in open air instead of using high heat, which can weaken elastic fibers.
