Size Guide

Find the right fit by brand and style.

Different brands fit differently. Select a brand to view its size chart, quick fit notes, and measurement help before you buy.

How to measure

Use a soft tape measure around bust, waist, and hips. Keep the tape level and snug, but not tight.

Fit by activity

Choose a performance fit for racing and training, or a relaxed fit for leisure swimwear and casual wear.

Fabric matters

Stretch fabrics usually feel more flexible, while compression styles feel firmer and more secure in the water.
