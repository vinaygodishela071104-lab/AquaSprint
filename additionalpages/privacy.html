Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AquaSprint collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you visit or shop on our website.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, billing address, and order details when you place an order, create an account, or contact us.

We may also collect technical information automatically, including your device type, browser type, IP address, pages visited, and interactions with the website.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to process and deliver orders, respond to your messages, provide customer support, improve website performance, and personalize your shopping experience.

We may also use information to send important service updates, order confirmations, or promotional messages if you have opted in to receive them.

3. Orders, Payments & Shipping

When you make a purchase, we use the information you provide to verify your order, process payment, arrange shipping, and complete delivery.

Payment details may be handled by secure third-party payment providers. AquaSprint does not store your full payment card details on our servers unless clearly stated during checkout.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and improve functionality.

Cookies help us understand how users browse the site, which pages are visited most, and how we can improve the shopping experience.

5. Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with trusted service providers who help us run the website, process payments, deliver products, or analyze site performance.

We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or business operations.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

However, no online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection of data transmitted over the internet.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, update, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information.

You may also request to stop receiving promotional messages by following the unsubscribe option or contacting us directly.

8. Children’s Privacy

AquaSprint does not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent. If we learn that such information has been provided, we will take steps to remove it.

9. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or website features.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact AquaSprint through the contact details listed on our website.
